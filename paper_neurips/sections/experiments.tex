\section{Experiments}
\label{sec:experiments}

We test one prediction from our theory: under fixed NFE, non-uniform allocation should beat uniform whenever per-step sensitivity varies, with gains growing in the dispersion of $\{\sigma_t\}$.

\paragraph{Setting.}
We evaluate \textsc{GAINS} on Stable Diffusion (SD; \citealp{rombach2022high}), EDM~\citep{karras2022elucidating}, and a flow-based model under strict NFE budgets, with two lightweight verifiers: \textbf{Brightness} (luminance) and \textbf{Compressibility} (JPEG size). The Uniform benchmark runs the same $\epsilon$-greedy noise search~\citep{ramesh2025nts} but allocates evenly, matching prior fixed-schedule choices~\citep{ramesh2025nts,ma2024inference}. \textsc{GAINS} replaces uniform with offline profiling + online windowed early stopping, redistributing saved NFE so the realized total exactly matches $B$. All runs use a single A100.

\paragraph{Hyperparameters and reproducibility.}
EDM ($T=18$): $\epsilon = 0.4$, $\lambda = 0.15$, $N = 4$, $\beta_g = 0.3$, $\beta_\sigma = 0.7$, $\delta = 2$. SD ($T=50$): $N = 4$, $\beta_g = 0.1$, $\beta_\sigma = 0.8$. All runs use the \emph{revert-on-negative} rule with $W_g = 4$; $\{K_t\}$ from offline profiling. Each configuration averages 20 seeds; SD prompt-set runs use 20 prompts $\times$ 10 repeats. Code released on acceptance.

\textsc{GAINS} improves both absolute scores and the \emph{rate} at which quality scales with NFE (\cref{tab:sd_budget,tab:edm_budget}). On SD, the Brightness margin widens from $+0.0223$ (400 NFE) to $+0.0321$ (800 NFE); \textsc{GAINS}/400 beats Uniform/500 (\textbf{20\%} fewer) and \textsc{GAINS}/400 Compressibility exceeds Uniform/800 (\textbf{50\%} fewer). On EDM, \textsc{GAINS}/144 matches or beats Uniform/288 (\textbf{50\%} / \textbf{37.5\%} fewer). The larger EDM margin tracks \cref{thm:offline}\textup{(iii)}: EDM's $\{\sigma_t\}$ concentrate on a middle segment, giving higher $\mathrm{Var}(\sigma_t)$ than SD's early-spread profile (\cref{sec:appendix-experiments}). Seed-to-seed std also tightens (e.g., $\pm 0.005$ vs.\ $\pm 0.015$ at EDM/144).

\subsection{Ablation, Generalization, Operator and Flow Compatibility}
\label{sec:exp-ablation}

Four stress tests (full tables in \cref{sec:appendix-experiments}) confirm the gains: \textbf{(a)~online vs.\ offline-only} (SD/400 NFE): online adds $+0.0090$/$+0.0073$ on Brightness/Compressibility, consistent with the Jensen-gap mechanism (\cref{rmk:online-value}); \textbf{(b)~generalization} (20 prompts $\times$ 10 repeats): Brightness $0.6949 \to 0.7213$ ($+0.0264$), Compressibility $0.7938 \to 0.8076$ ($+0.0138$); \textbf{(c)~operator compatibility}: gains hold under both zero-order and random search local operators (\cref{rmk:separation}); \textbf{(d)~flow-based models}: after stochasticity injection~\citep{dockhorn2024stochastic,kim2025rbf}, \textsc{GAINS} adds $+0.005$ to $+0.015$ across $\{80,100,150\}$ NFE (vs.\ $+0.02$ to $+0.04$ on SD/EDM), consistent with narrower $\{\sigma_t\}$ dispersion after ODE-to-SDE conversion (\cref{sec:appendix-flow}).
