\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We addressed inference-time NFE allocation, shifting the question from \emph{how} to search at one step to \emph{where} along the trajectory. The two-level framework (\cref{sec:framework}) recovers plain sampling, best-of-$N$, $\epsilon$-greedy, and particle methods as special cases; \textsc{GAINS} pairs offline sensitivity profiling with online feedback, with leading-order water-filling (\cref{thm:offline}) and an $\Omega(T)$ regret bound (\cref{lem:lower}) justifying the hybrid design. Empirically, \textsc{GAINS} matches uniform with 20--50\% fewer NFE on SD/EDM/flow models. \emph{Limitations.} Leading-order in $g_t\sqrt h$, $\Psi_t \in C^2$; the MDP formulation in \cref{sec:mdp} opens learned schedulers and multi-verifier composition.
