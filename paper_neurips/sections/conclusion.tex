\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We shifted inference-time compute allocation from \emph{how} to search at one step to \emph{where} along the trajectory. The two-level framework (\cref{sec:framework}) recovers plain sampling, best-of-$N$, $\epsilon$-greedy, and particle methods as special cases; \textsc{GAINS} pairs offline profiling with online feedback, with water-filling (\cref{thm:offline}) and an $\Omega(T)$ regret bound (\cref{lem:lower}) justifying the hybrid; it matches uniform with 20--50\% fewer NFE on SD/EDM/flow. \emph{Limitations:} leading-order in $g_t\sqrt h$ with global $C^{1,1}$ smoothness (excluding non-smooth verifiers); \cref{sec:mdp} opens learned schedulers.
