# Price of Robustness Sentence Templates

Sentence patterns for robust optimization tradeoffs and uncertainty handling papers.

---

## [Historical Context] A [approach] to solving [problem type] with [challenge] was proposed in [period] and has recently been extensively studied and extended.

**Source**: Bertsimas & Sim - "The Price of Robustness" (OR 2004)
**Context**: Introducing established approach with recent developments

> A robust approach to solving linear optimization problems with uncertain data was proposed in the early 1970s and has recently been extensively studied and extended.

**Tags**: #history #context #robust_optimization #introduction

---

## [Tradeoff Motivation] Under this approach, we are willing to accept a [suboptimal outcome] for the [nominal case] in order to ensure that the solution remains [desirable property] when the [uncertainty] changes.

**Source**: Bertsimas & Sim - "The Price of Robustness" (OR 2004)
**Context**: Explaining the fundamental tradeoff in robust approaches

> Under this approach, we are willing to accept a suboptimal solution for the nominal values of the data in order to ensure that the solution remains feasible and near optimal when the data changes.

**Tags**: #tradeoff #motivation #robust_optimization

---

## [Conservatism Concern] A concern with such an approach is that it might be too [extreme property]. In this paper, we propose an approach that attempts to make this trade-off more attractive.

**Source**: Bertsimas & Sim - "The Price of Robustness" (OR 2004)
**Context**: Motivating new contribution by addressing limitation of existing approach

> A concern with such an approach is that it might be too conservative. In this paper, we propose an approach that attempts to make this trade-off more attractive; that is, we investigate ways to decrease what we call the price of robustness.

**Tags**: #motivation #limitation #contribution #robust_optimization

## [Flexibility Claim] We flexibly adjust the level of [property A] in terms of [property B]. An attractive aspect of our method is that [tractability claim].

**Source**: Bertsimas & Sim - "The Price of Robustness" (OR 2004)
**Context**: Stating contribution with tractability guarantee

> We flexibly adjust the level of conservatism of the robust solutions in terms of probabilistic bounds of constraint violations. An attractive aspect of our method is that the new robust formulation is also a linear optimization problem.

## [Definition] The difference between the [optimal robust quantity] and the [nominal quantity] is called the [concept name]. It is useful if [condition], since in that case [interpretation].

**Source**: Bertsimas & Sim - "The Price of Robustness" (OR 2004)
**Context**: Defining key concept with interpretation

> The difference between the optimal robust objective and the nominal solution is called the price of robustness (PoR). It is useful if the objective is certain, since in that case PoR is the amount that has to be paid for being robust against constraint violations.
