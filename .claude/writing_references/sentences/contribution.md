# Contribution Statement Templates

Sentence patterns for clearly stating contributions.

---

## [Numbered Contributions]

**Source**: NeurIPS/ICML best papers
**Context**: Listing multiple contributions

> Our contributions can be summarized as follows:
> - First, we formalize [problem] and identify [key insight].
> - Second, we propose [algorithm] that [property].
> - Third, we provide [theoretical guarantee].
> - Finally, we validate our approach on [experiments].

**Tags**: #contribution #structure #comprehensive

---

## [Single Main Contribution]

**Source**: Focused theoretical papers
**Context**: Emphasizing one major contribution

> The main contribution of this work is to establish [result], which [significance].

> Our primary contribution is a novel [method/framework] for [problem].

> We contribute a complete characterization of [phenomenon].

**Tags**: #contribution #focused #theory

---

## [Bridging Contribution]

**Source**: Interdisciplinary papers
**Context**: Connecting different fields

> This work bridges [field A] and [field B] by [connection].

> We unify the perspectives of [approach A] and [approach B] under [framework].

> Our framework provides a common lens through which to view [disparate problems].

**Tags**: #contribution #bridging #unification

---

## [Practical Contribution]

**Source**: Applied papers
**Context**: Emphasizing practical impact

> From a practical standpoint, our method enables [application].

> Our approach reduces [cost/time/resources] by [amount] while maintaining [quality].

> We demonstrate that [technique] can be deployed at scale in [setting].

**Tags**: #contribution #practical #application

---

## [Theoretical Contribution]

**Source**: Theory papers
**Context**: Highlighting theoretical advances

> On the theoretical side, we prove [theorem] which [implication].

> Our analysis reveals a fundamental connection between [A] and [B].

> We settle the question of [open problem] by showing [result].

> Our lower bound establishes that [limit], demonstrating the optimality of [method].

**Tags**: #contribution #theoretical #proof

---

## [Negative/Surprising Results]

**Source**: Papers with counterintuitive findings
**Context**: Highlighting unexpected results

> Perhaps surprisingly, we show that [counterintuitive result].

> Contrary to conventional wisdom, our analysis reveals that [finding].

> We provide a counterexample showing that [common belief] does not hold in [setting].

**Tags**: #contribution #surprising #negative
