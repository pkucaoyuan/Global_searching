## [Formal Definition] A [model], as defined by [citations], is defined as a tuple M = (X, A, p, r) where X is the [state space] and A is the [action space].

**Source**: Puterman (1994) and MDP textbooks
**Context**: Formal model definition

> A Markov decision process, as defined by Bellman (1957), Howard (1960), and Puterman (1994), is defined as a tuple M = (X, A, p, r) where X is the state space and A is the action space.
