# Motivation Sentence Templates

Sentence patterns for motivating research problems.

---

## [Practical Motivation] It has become standard practice for [practitioners] to [action]

**Source**: Howard et al. - "Time-uniform confidence sequences" (Annals of Statistics 2021)
**Context**: Opening with real-world relevance

> It has become standard practice for organizations with online presence to run large-scale randomized experiments, or "A/B tests", to improve product performance and user experience.

**Variants**:
- It is increasingly common for [practitioners] to...
- Modern [field] relies heavily on...
- With the proliferation of [technology], practitioners routinely...

**Tags**: #motivation #practical #opening #howard

---

## [Sequential Nature] Such [phenomena] are inherently sequential

**Source**: Howard et al. - Annals of Statistics 2021
**Context**: Explaining why sequential methods matter

> Such experiments are inherently sequential: visitors arrive in a stream and outcomes are typically observed quickly relative to the duration of the test.

**Pattern**:
> [Domain] is inherently [property]: [explanation of why].

**Tags**: #motivation #sequential #characterization #howard

---

## [Current Practice Problem] Results are often [handled] using [methods] that assume [assumption], despite the known problem that [issue]

**Source**: Howard et al. - Annals of Statistics 2021
**Context**: Critiquing common practice

> Results are often monitored continuously using inferential methods that assume a fixed sample, despite the known problem that such monitoring inflates Type I error substantially.

**Variants**:
- Standard practice involves [method], even though [problem]...
- Practitioners commonly rely on [approach], which fails when [condition]...

**Tags**: #motivation #problem #critique #howard

---

## [Gap: Formal vs Informal] Most [tasks] are run with little formal [property] compared to [benchmark]

**Source**: Howard et al. - Annals of Statistics 2021
**Context**: Contrasting current practice with ideal

> Furthermore, most A/B tests are run with little formal planning and fluid decision-making, compared to clinical trials or industrial quality control, the traditional applications of sequential analysis.

**Tags**: #motivation #gap #contrast #howard

---

## [Paper Contribution Intro] This paper presents methods for [goal] as a flexible tool for [application]

**Source**: Howard et al. - Annals of Statistics 2021
**Context**: Introducing paper's contribution

> This paper presents methods for deriving confidence sequences as a flexible tool for inference in sequential experiments.

**Variants**:
- We develop techniques for [X] that enable [Y]...
- Our work introduces [method] as a principled approach to [problem]...

**Tags**: #contribution #introduction #method #howard

---

## [Formal Definition with Property] For [parameter], a [object] is a [mathematical structure] satisfying [property]

**Source**: Howard et al. - Annals of Statistics 2021
**Context**: Formally introducing a concept

> For $\alpha \in (0,1)$, a $(1-\alpha)$-confidence sequence is a sequence of confidence sets $(CI_t)_{t=1}^\infty$, typically intervals $CI_t = (L_t, U_t) \subseteq \R$, satisfying a uniform coverage guarantee.

**Tags**: #definition #formal #property #howard

---

## [Technique Generality] We illustrate the generality of our proof techniques by deriving [result A], as well as [result B]

**Source**: Howard et al. - Annals of Statistics 2021
**Context**: Highlighting versatility of methods

> We illustrate the generality of our proof techniques by deriving an empirical-Bernstein bound growing at a LIL rate, as well as a novel upper LIL for the maximum eigenvalue of a sum of random matrices.

**Tags**: #contribution #generality #technique #howard

**Context**: Previewing applications
