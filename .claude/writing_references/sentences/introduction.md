# Introduction Sentence Templates

Sentence patterns for paper introductions from top venues.

---

## [Opening - Importance]

**Source**: Common OR/ML paper openings
**Context**: Establishing topic importance

> [Topic] has emerged as a fundamental challenge in [field].

> The problem of [X] lies at the heart of [application domain].

> Understanding [phenomenon] is crucial for [practical goal].

**Tags**: #introduction #opening #importance

---

## [Opening - Present State]

**Source**: Prof JD edits (Feb 2026) — preferred over progressive-aspect openings
**Context**: Stating current reality directly, no temporal vagueness

> [Technology/systems] now serve as [role] that must handle [challenge] under [constraints].

> [Domain] relies on [mechanism], yet [mechanism] creates [fundamental tension].

**Anti-patterns** (avoid these vague temporal openings):
- ~~"are increasingly deployed as"~~ → "now serve as"
- ~~"has emerged as a primary bottleneck"~~ → "constrains"
- ~~"With the rapid growth of"~~ → state the current fact directly

**Tags**: #introduction #opening #present_tense #advisor

---

## [Opening - Growth]

**Source**: Applied ML papers
**Context**: Highlighting growing relevance (use sparingly — prefer Present State above)

> With the rapid growth of [technology/data], there is an increasing need for [solution].

> Recent advances in [field] have enabled [new capability], yet [challenge] remains.

> The proliferation of [X] has created unprecedented demand for [Y].

**Tags**: #introduction #opening #trend

---

## [Gap Identification]

**Source**: Methodology papers
**Context**: Identifying research gaps

> Despite significant progress, existing approaches suffer from [limitation].

> While prior work has focused on [A], the question of [B] remains largely unexplored.

> A key limitation of current methods is their inability to [handle X].

> However, these methods typically assume [unrealistic assumption], which [problem].

**Tags**: #introduction #gap #motivation

---

## [Contribution Statement]

**Source**: Top conference papers
**Context**: Stating contributions clearly

> In this paper, we propose [method name], a novel approach that [key innovation].

> Our main contribution is a [type of contribution] that [benefit].

> We make the following contributions: (i) [first], (ii) [second], (iii) [third].

> This work bridges the gap between [A] and [B] by [approach].

**Tags**: #introduction #contribution #claim

---

## [Technical Approach Preview]

**Source**: Theoretical papers
**Context**: Previewing the technical approach

> Our approach builds on [foundation] and extends it to [new setting].

> The key insight underlying our method is that [insight].

> We achieve this by [technique], which allows us to [benefit].

> At a high level, our algorithm proceeds by [brief description].

**Tags**: #introduction #approach #preview

---

## [Results Preview]

**Source**: Empirical papers
**Context**: Previewing key results

> Our theoretical analysis establishes that [main theorem statement].

> Experiments on [benchmarks] demonstrate that our method [key finding].

> We prove that [algorithm] achieves [bound], matching the information-theoretic lower bound.

> Our results show an improvement of [X]% over the state-of-the-art.

**Tags**: #introduction #results #preview
