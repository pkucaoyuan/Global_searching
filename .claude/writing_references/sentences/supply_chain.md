# Supply Chain Management Sentence Templates

Sentence patterns for supply chain, operations management, and coordination papers.

## [Phenomenon Definition] The [effect] is a supply chain phenomenon where [upstream quantity] tends to have a larger variability than [downstream quantity], resulting in [consequence].

**Source**: Lee et al. - "The Bullwhip Effect in Supply Chains" (MS 1997)
**Context**: Defining key phenomenon

> The bullwhip effect is a supply chain phenomenon where orders to suppliers tend to have a larger variability than sales to buyers, which results in an amplified demand variability upstream.

**Tags**: #definition #bullwhip #phenomenon #supply_chain

**Source**: Lee et al. (MS 1997)
**Context**: Describing information distortion

## [Information Sharing] Information sharing across the supply chain is an effective strategy to mitigate the [problem]. For example, [company] transmits [data type] from [source] back to [destination].

**Source**: Supply chain case studies
**Context**: Illustrating best practice

> Information sharing across the supply chain is an effective strategy to mitigate the bullwhip effect. For example, Wal-Mart transmits point-of-sale (POS) data from individual stores back to corporate headquarters several times a day.
