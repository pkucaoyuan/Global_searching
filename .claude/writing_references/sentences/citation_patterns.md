# Citation Patterns

Sentence patterns for citing and discussing prior work.

---

## [Terminology History] The idea of [concept] goes back at least to [Author]

**Source**: Howard et al. - Annals of Statistics 2021
**Context**: Tracing historical origins

> The idea of a confidence sequence goes back at least to Darling and Robbins (1967).

**Variants**:
- This concept was first introduced by...
- The origins of [X] can be traced to...
- [X] has a rich history dating back to...

**Tags**: #citation #history #origin #howard

---

## [Alternative Names] They are called [name A] by [Author A] and [name B] by [Author B]

**Source**: Howard et al. - Annals of Statistics 2021
**Context**: Noting terminological variations

> They are called repeated confidence intervals by Jennison and Turnbull (1984) (with a focus on finite time horizons) and always-valid confidence intervals by Johari et al. (2015). They are sometimes labeled anytime confidence intervals in the machine learning literature.

**Tags**: #citation #terminology #alternatives #howard

## [Duality Statement] We show in [reference] that [definition A] is equivalent to [definition B]

**Source**: Howard et al. - Annals of Statistics 2021
**Context**: Establishing equivalence between formulations
