# Queueing Theory Sentence Templates

Sentence patterns for queueing theory and service operations papers.

## [Foundational Regime] [Authors] extended this analysis by examining a heavy-traffic asymptotic regime where [parameter] approaches [limit], while maintaining [constraint].

**Source**: Papers citing Halfin & Whitt (OR 1981)
**Context**: Describing asymptotic regime

> Halfin and Whitt extended this analysis by examining a heavy-traffic asymptotic regime where the customer arrival rate approaches infinity, while maintaining a fixed, unit-normalized service rate per agent.

**Tags**: #regime #heavy_traffic #asymptotic #queueing

---

## [Square-Root Staffing] The staffing rule is [formula]. This is the square-root safety rule. Its asymptotic optimality has been established in [regime] across various settings.

**Source**: Halfin-Whitt literature
**Context**: Stating key staffing rule

> The staffing rule is λ + β√λ. This is the square-root safety rule. Its asymptotic optimality has been established in a heavy-traffic regime as λ → ∞ across various settings.

**Tags**: #staffing #square_root #rule #queueing

## [Target Quality] Achieving a target [performance metric] requires staffing levels to exceed the nominal load, defined as [definition].

**Source**: Halfin-Whitt literature
**Context**: Explaining staffing requirement

> Achieving a target delay probability requires staffing levels to exceed the nominal load, defined as the arrival-to-service rate ratio. The nominal load represents the theoretical minimum staffing needed for delay-free service in an idealized system without uncertainty.

**Tags**: #staffing #target #nominal_load #queueing
