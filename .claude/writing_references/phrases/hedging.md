# Hedging Phrases

Academic hedging expressions for cautious, precise claims.

---

## [Possibility] May / Might / Could

**Source**: Standard in theoretical claims
**Context**: Expressing possibility without overclaiming

> This observation may suggest that...
> The improvement might be attributed to...
> This could potentially lead to...

**Tags**: #hedging #possibility #modal

---

## [Tendency] Tend to / Generally / Typically

**Source**: Empirical observations
**Context**: Describing patterns without absolute claims

> The algorithm tends to converge faster when...
> Generally, larger sample sizes yield...
> Performance typically improves as...

**Tags**: #hedging #tendency #empirical

---

## [Approximation] Approximately / Roughly / On the order of

**Source**: Quantitative results
**Context**: Numerical claims with uncertainty

> The complexity is approximately O(n log n)...
> Roughly speaking, we expect...
> The improvement is on the order of 10%...

**Tags**: #hedging #approximation #quantitative

---

## [Indication] Suggest / Indicate / Point to

**Source**: Results interpretation
**Context**: Drawing conclusions from evidence

> These results suggest that...
> Our findings indicate...
> The evidence points to...

**Variants**:
- Imply
- Support the hypothesis that
- Are consistent with

**Tags**: #hedging #indication #results

---

## [Limitation] Under certain conditions / In some cases

**Source**: Methodology sections
**Context**: Qualifying scope of claims

> Under certain regularity conditions...
> In some cases, the bound may be loose...
> For a restricted class of problems...

**Variants**:
- Given appropriate assumptions
- When the conditions are met
- Subject to the constraints

**Tags**: #hedging #limitation #scope

---

## [Uncertainty] It appears that / Seemingly

**Source**: Discussion sections
**Context**: Tentative observations

> It appears that the method is robust to...
> Seemingly, this approach outperforms...
> The pattern appears to hold across...

**Tags**: #hedging #uncertainty #observation

---

## [Attribution] According to / Based on

**Source**: Literature review
**Context**: Attributing claims to sources

> According to the analysis in [X]...
> Based on the framework of [Y]...
> Following the approach of [Z]...

**Tags**: #hedging #attribution #citation
