# Transition Phrases

Academic transition phrases from human-authored papers.

---

## [Addition] Furthermore / Moreover

**Source**: Common in top OR journals
**Context**: Adding supporting points

> Furthermore, this approach enables...
> Moreover, our analysis reveals...

**Variants**:
- In addition
- Additionally
- What is more

**Tags**: #transition #addition #formal

---

## [Contrast] However / Nevertheless

**Source**: Standard academic usage
**Context**: Introducing contrasting information

> However, existing methods fail to...
> Nevertheless, the theoretical guarantees remain...

**Variants**:
- In contrast
- On the other hand
- Yet
- Nonetheless

**Tags**: #transition #contrast #formal

---

## [Consequence] Consequently / As a result

**Source**: Common in methodology sections
**Context**: Showing cause-effect relationships

> Consequently, the estimator achieves...
> As a result, we observe a significant improvement...

**Variants**:
- Therefore
- Thus
- Hence
- Accordingly

**Tags**: #transition #consequence #formal

---

## [Sequence] Subsequently / Following this

**Source**: Algorithm descriptions
**Context**: Describing sequential steps

> Subsequently, the algorithm updates...
> Following this observation, we propose...

**Variants**:
- Next
- Then
- Afterward
- In the following step

**Tags**: #transition #sequence #methodology

---

## [Example] For instance / To illustrate

**Source**: Theoretical papers
**Context**: Providing concrete examples

> For instance, consider the case where...
> To illustrate, suppose that...

**Variants**:
- As an example
- Consider the following
- Specifically

**Tags**: #transition #example #exposition

---

## [Summary] In summary / To summarize

**Source**: Conclusion sections
**Context**: Wrapping up arguments

> In summary, our contributions are threefold...
> To summarize, we have established that...

**Variants**:
- In conclusion
- To conclude
- Overall
- Taken together

**Tags**: #transition #summary #conclusion

---

## [Emphasis] Notably / Importantly

**Source**: Results sections
**Context**: Highlighting key findings

> Notably, our method achieves state-of-the-art...
> Importantly, this result holds even when...

**Variants**:
- Crucially
- Significantly
- Remarkably
- It is worth noting that

**Tags**: #transition #emphasis #results
