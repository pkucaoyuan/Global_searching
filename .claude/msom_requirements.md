# MSOM (M&SOM) Paper Submission Requirements

> **Source**: [MSOM Submission Guidelines](https://pubsonline.informs.org/page/msom/submission-guidelines) | [Call for Papers](https://pubsonline.informs.org/page/msom/calls-for-papers)

## Journal Information

- **Full Name**: Manufacturing & Service Operations Management (M&SOM)
- **Publisher**: INFORMS
- **Frequency**: Bimonthly
- **Review Type**: Double-anonymous
- **Submission System**: https://mc.manuscriptcentral.com/msom

---

## Editorial Scope

M&SOM publishes research on the production and operations management of goods and services, including:

- Technology management
- Productivity and quality management
- Product development and innovation
- Cross-functional coordination
- Practice-based research
- **AI/ML in operations** (growing area of interest)

### Key Evaluation Criteria

1. **Originality**: New insights for OM theory or practice
2. **Contribution**: Advances understanding of operations management
3. **Scientific merit**: Rigorous methodology
4. **Operational relevance**: Clear implications for how operations are managed

## Page Limits (Strict)

| Section | Limit | Notes |
|---------|-------|-------|
| **Total manuscript** | **32 pages max** | Includes ALL content below |
| — Main body | Within 32 pages | Introduction through conclusion |
| — References | Within 32 pages | Author-year format |
| — Tables & Figures | Within 32 pages | Embedded in text |
| — Appendices | Within 32 pages | If included in main file |
| **Online supplement** | **16 pages max** | Separate file, for new submissions after Jan 1, 2021 |

> **Warning**: The 32-page limit includes EVERYTHING — references, tables, figures, appendices. Budget carefully.

---

## Formatting Requirements

| Requirement | Specification |
|-------------|--------------|
| Spacing | **Double-spaced** throughout (max 33 lines/page) |
| Includes | Abstract, main body, references, figures, tables, appendices |
| Template | MSOM LaTeX or Word style file from INFORMS |
| File format | PDF upload |
| Footnotes | **PROHIBITED** — incorporate into main text in parentheses |
| Citations | Author-year: (Norman 1977) or Norman (1977) |
| Anonymity | **Double-anonymous** — no author names, affiliations, or acknowledgments |

---

## Anonymity Requirements

- Remove all author names and affiliations
- Remove acknowledgments
- Avoid self-identifying references (e.g., "In our previous work [Author, Year]...")
- Use third-person when citing own work: "Author (Year) showed..." not "We previously showed..."

---

## AI Policy

> Authors are welcome to use generative artificial intelligence (AI) tools in the preparation of their manuscripts. However, such tools may not be listed as authors.

| Dimension | MSOM | MS | ICML |
|-----------|------|----|------|
| Page limit | **32 pp (all-inclusive)** | Flexible (~25-40 pp) | 8 pp + unlimited appendix |
| Supplement | 16 pp max | Unlimited e-companion | Unlimited appendix |

## Practical Budget Advice for a 32-Page Paper

Given the hard 32-page limit (double-spaced), plan allocation carefully:

| Section | Recommended Pages | Notes |
|---------|------------------|-------|
| Abstract | 0.5 | Must fit on first page |
| Introduction | 3-4 | Operational motivation + contributions |
| Related Work | 2-3 | OM + AI/ML + AI-in-ops |
| Problem Setup / Model | 3-4 | With operational interpretation |
| Methodology | 5-7 | Core technical content |
| Experiments | 6-8 | Central for MSOM |
| Discussion / Conclusion | 2-3 | Practical implications |
| References | 2-3 | ~40-60 references typical |
| Tables & Figures | 3-5 | Embedded in text |
| **Total** | **~27-32** | Stay within limit |

### What Goes in the Online Supplement (16 pp max)

- Full proofs and derivations
- Additional experimental results
- Extended tables
- Implementation details
- Robustness checks
- Data description details

## Key Style Differences from ML Papers

For authors coming from ML/CS venues:

1. **No footnotes** — this is a hard rule, not a suggestion
2. **Operational framing** — every technical contribution must be connected to operational practice
3. **Double-spaced** — your 8-page ICML paper becomes ~20 pages in MSOM format
4. **Inclusive page count** — references and figures count toward the limit
5. **Author-year citations** — not numbered references
6. **Practical implications** — "so what?" must be answered for practitioners
7. **OM literature** — must engage with the operations management literature, not just ML/AI
